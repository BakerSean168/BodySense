This file is an adapted work. BodySense modified the Z-Anatomy material:
- selected the visible skeletal muscles and their tendons/aponeuroses from the
  Z-Anatomy muscular system (deep, visceral, facial-internal and fascia parts left out);
- split each structure into muscle and tendon parts, applied world transforms,
  triangulated and decimated the meshes, and dropped materials and UVs;
- exported the result as one flat glTF 2.0 binary in Z-Anatomy world space (Y-up).
Tool: Blender 4.2 headless, scripts/anatomy/z-anatomy-muscles-v1.export.py.
